About Stay Alive Bible Study

Deep faith and consistent prayers.

Bridging the Gap (Not an Official Church Website)

Please note that this is not an official church website, but rather an independent digital extension and tool created specifically to bridge the gap in the Bible study area. It ensures that members never miss out on teachings, notes, and study guides when they are unable to attend physical sessions.

Important Note: This platform does not serve as a replacement for physical fellowship, which we strongly recommend and encourage for every member.

The Bible study is coordinated by Antony Mutahi, Dennis White, and Grace Muthoni, operating under the leadership of our youth chairman, Timothy Munene.

Deep Faith

The foundation of our teaching tracks and resource archive. We dive deep into scripture, theological roots, and books of the gospel to mature believers in Christ.

Consistent Prayers

Driven by our midweek prayer commitments and intercession walls, keeping our fellowship spiritually vigilant, unified, and anchored before God.
